Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Iconico Hair (“we”, “us”, “the App”) collects, uses, and protects personal information when you use our iOS/Android application and related booking services at iconicohair.it.

1. Data controller

Iconico Hair
Website: https://iconicohair.it
API / services: https://api.iconicohair.it
Contact: use the in-app Contact us form, or email the salon support address provided by the shop.

2. Information we collect

  • Account data: name, email, phone (if provided), password (stored securely by our backend).
  • Profile data: optional profile photo/avatar.
  • Booking data: shop, service, barber, date/time, recurring preferences, notes.
  • Support messages: content you submit via the contact form.
  • Technical data: device/app diagnostics needed to operate the service (e.g. authentication tokens, basic request logs).

3. How we use your information

  • Create and manage your account
  • Book, display, update, and cancel appointments
  • Communicate about bookings and support requests
  • Improve reliability and security of the App
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal data.

4. Legal bases (GDPR)

Where applicable (e.g. users in the EU/EEA/UK/Italy), processing is based on:

  • Performance of a contract (providing booking services)
  • Legitimate interests (security, service improvement)
  • Consent (where required, e.g. optional profile photo)
  • Legal obligations

5. Sharing

Booking details are shared with the selected salon/barbers as needed to fulfill your appointment. We may use infrastructure providers (hosting, APIs) strictly to operate the service. We do not share data with advertisers for cross-app tracking in this App.

6. Retention

We retain account and booking data for as long as your account is active and as needed for legal, accounting, or dispute purposes. You may request deletion (see below).

7. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or portability of your data.

To delete your account and related personal data, visit: Account deletion instructions.

8. Children

The App is intended for general audiences / adult customers booking salon services. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. Parents/guardians who believe a child provided data should contact us to request deletion.

9. Security

We use industry-standard measures such as HTTPS and authenticated API access. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date will change when we do. Continued use of the App after updates means you accept the revised policy.

11. Contact

For privacy questions, use Contact us in the App or reach the salon via iconicohair.it.
