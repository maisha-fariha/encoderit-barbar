Account deletion

Last updated: 23 September 2026

Apple and Google require a way for users to request deletion of their account and associated data. This page explains how to request deletion for Iconico Hair.

How to request deletion

  1. Open the Iconico Hair app and sign in with the account you want deleted.
  2. Go to Contact us (or Profile → contact/support).
  3. Send a message with subject “Delete my account” and include the email address registered on your account.
  4. Alternatively, email the salon from the same email used in the app, with subject “Delete my Iconico Hair account”.

What we delete

After verifying your identity, we will delete or anonymize:

  • Account credentials and profile information (name, email, phone, avatar)
  • App session / authentication data tied to your account
  • Booking history associated with your user account, where not legally required to retain
  • Support messages linked to your account, where feasible

What may be retained

  • Records required by law (e.g. tax/accounting) for the legally required period
  • Security logs needed to prevent fraud/abuse, for a limited time
  • Anonymous / aggregated analytics that cannot identify you

Timeline

We aim to process verified deletion requests within 30 days (often sooner). You will receive confirmation when deletion is completed, when possible.

App Store / Play Store note

This web page is the official account-deletion instruction URL for Iconico Hair. Deleting the app from your device does not automatically delete your account on our servers. You must submit a deletion request as described above.
